# Key Business Insights

## Revenue Insights
- Large pizzas generated the highest revenue contribution.
- A small number of pizzas contributed major sales revenue.

## Customer Ordering Behavior
- Customers mostly ordered during lunch and evening hours.
- Certain pizza sizes were ordered more frequently.

## Product Performance
- Some pizza categories consistently outperformed others.
- Top-selling pizzas dominated total orders.

## Business Recommendations
- Promote high-performing pizzas more aggressively.
- Optimize staffing during peak order hours.
- Increase focus on popular pizza categories.
